Philosophy & Focus

Distinctive identity design engineered to command presence.

I am John Ondwari Obiko, an international logo and brand identity designer based in Nairobi, Kenya and collaborating with forward-thinking brands worldwide. Over a decade of focused practice, I have brought more than 350 design projects from foundational concept to memorable global presence.

Great identities are born where analytical clarity meets visual restraint. By peeling back unnecessary decoration, I uncover the essential soul of each business — translating purpose, ambition, and character into timeless marks and cohesive design systems that build lasting recognition.
